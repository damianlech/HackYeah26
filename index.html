Clearance · an AI control layer for agents

No AI request leaves the firm without clearance.

Admins build a pipeline of gates: identity, budget, word filters, an AI judge. Every agent request is checked, rewritten or stopped, and every decision is on the record.

Four possible answers

  • Allowpasses it on
  • Denystops the line
  • Modifyrewrites, then passes on
  • Flagpasses, marked for review
